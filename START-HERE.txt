DEX ART CARD TRACKER - START HERE
=================================

What it is: a web page on your own computer that lists every Pokémon with its
cheapest art card (picture + price), and lets you tick off what you own.
It keeps its own card database and updates prices slowly in the background.


ONE-TIME SETUP (about 10 minutes)
---------------------------------
1. Install Docker Desktop (free for personal use):
   https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/
   Open it once after installing and wait until it says it is running.
   (Windows may ask you to restart. That is normal.)

2. Unzip this folder somewhere you will find it again, for example Documents.


STARTING IT
-----------
Windows: double-click  start.bat
Mac:     double-click  start.command
         (if the Mac refuses: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again)

Or, in any terminal opened inside this folder, type:
   docker compose up -d --build

The first start downloads some files and takes a few minutes.
Then open this address in your browser:

   http://localhost:8080


THE FIRST DAYS
--------------
The first time, the page says it is building its card database. That is
normal: it asks the card service for sets and art cards very slowly (one request
every 2 seconds, no daily limit) so it never overloads it. This takes a few
days to complete. You can close the page and come back later. Cards appear as
they are fetched, newest sets first.
After that, new sets are checked at start and once a day, and prices of the cheapest art
cards are refreshed slowly, a little each day.


LOGGING IN
----------
Everyone who opens the page sees a view-only version. Every trainer has their
own dex: pick it in the "Dex of" menu at the top right, then click the blue lens
at the top left and enter that dex's password. A login only works on that dex.

You are the main admin. Your password is printed in the container log (look for
OWNER PASSWORD) and saved as admin.json in the data folder. Or choose your own
with the ADMIN_PASSWORD setting in docker-compose.yml.
To give someone their own dex: log in, open the "Dex of" menu and choose
"+ Add account...". "Manage accounts..." gives a new password or deletes one.
Everyone can rename their own dex with the "Trainer name" button and change
their own password with the "Password" button.
Friends can use http://<address>:8080/view/ : other trainers can log in to their
own dex there, but the main admin login never works on that address.


USING IT
--------
- Each Pokémon has a light: red = nothing yet, yellow = you have a card,
  green = you have the art card.
- "Any card" and "Art card" buttons: tick what you own. Ticks are saved on
  the server, so every device sees them.
- The red menu at the start of the bar switches between Pokémon, Sets,
  Trainers, Wish list & trades, and History.
- "view" on a Pokémon: see all its art cards and regular cards, tick the ones
  you have, and choose "Wish for this one" to put a card on your wish list.
  Cards in the Sets and Trainers views have a "Wish" button too.
- "Wish list & trades": your wish list with its total price and who owns those
  cards, and the cards you have that other trainers wish for.
- "History": the cards you ticked most recently.
- The sort menu on the Pokémon view lists the Pokémon you own a card of by
  price, high to low or low to high.
- "Sets": every set with your progress. Open one to see all its cards and
  tick the ones you own.
- Click any picture to see it large.
- The server stores its own copy of every card picture (low and high
  resolution), so the page keeps working when the picture sites are down.
  Filling the store takes a day or two and roughly 3-5 GB of disk space.
- Cards without a picture (mostly promos) are looked up automatically in the
  background. One that is still missing shows a Poké Ball with the card's name;
  the main admin can give it a picture with the "Picture..." button on the card
  (paste a picture address or choose a file).
- If a card is wrongly counted (or not counted) as an art card, the main admin
  can use "Count as art card" / "Not an art card" on that card.
- Prices are shown in euro (Cardmarket). For dollar (TCGplayer) prices, set
  PRICE_SOURCE: "tcgplayer" in docker-compose.yml and start it again.
- Once you own the art card of a Pokémon, its price is no longer updated.


APP AND SCANNING
----------------
On an Android phone, open the page over https (your reverse proxy address) in
Chrome and press "Install app" at the top right. Log in to your dex, tap the
blue lens at the top left, hold a card in view (or lay several side by side)
and tap the big lens under the picture: the
best matches appear for every card, tap "Have it" or "Wish" on the right one.
"Use a photo" scans a saved picture.


STOPPING AND STARTING AGAIN
---------------------------
Stop:   docker compose down      (your data and ticks are kept)
Start:  double-click start.bat / start.command again

Docker Desktop must be running whenever you want to use the page.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "docker is not recognized" / "command not found": Docker Desktop is not
  installed or not running. Open Docker Desktop and wait for it to start.
- The page does not open: check the address is exactly http://localhost:8080
- Port 8080 already in use: open docker-compose.yml in a text editor and
  change "8080:8080" to "8081:8080", then use http://localhost:8081
- Page stays empty for a long time: see what the server is doing with
     docker compose logs --tail 50
  and send me those lines.
- No art cards appear but the log shows cards were fetched: the rarity name
  may differ. Send me the log and I will adjust the RARITIES setting.

Other devices on your home network can use the page too:
http://<this computer's address>:8080  (passwords are sent without https, so
do not expose it to the internet without an https proxy or VPN).

More settings are described in README.md.
